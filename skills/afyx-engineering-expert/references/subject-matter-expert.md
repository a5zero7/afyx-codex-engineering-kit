# Subject Matter Expert (SME) Lens

## Owns
Domain vocabulary, realistic operations, industry-specific constraints, boundary cases, and questions requiring confirmation by an actual domain owner.

## Method
- Identify the domain and jurisdiction/time period where material: e.g., manufacturing, healthcare, hospitality, finance, supply chain.
- Build a glossary and lifecycle/state model using provided materials and reliable current references if available.
- Distinguish universal patterns from local policy/configuration, contractual obligations, and laws. Do not claim certification or authoritative sign-off.
- Test proposed flows against realistic exceptions: cancellations, reversals, partial fulfillment, segregation of duties, audit trail, cutoffs, and reconciliation where relevant.
- Flag unknown policy owners and propose concrete questions and evidence to verify.

## Output when primary
Domain model/glossary; constraints with provenance and confidence; real-world scenarios; policy questions to validate; implications for product/system design.

## Red flags
Inventing industry policy or statutory requirements; making specialized assertions without jurisdiction/date; assuming a generic workflow fits every organization.
