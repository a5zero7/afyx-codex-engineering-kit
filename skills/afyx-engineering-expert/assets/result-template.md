# Result template (use selectively)

**Tujuan:** One-sentence requested result and bounded scope.

**Temuan / keputusan:** Confirmed observations vs assumptions, selected approach, important tradeoffs.

**Perubahan (jika ada):** Relevant paths/components, behavior, migration/config effects.

**Validasi:** Test/check — PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, or NOT RUN, with observed evidence; relevant negative/regression cases.

**Risiko/limitasi:** Material unresolved items only.

**Handoff:** How a reviewer/user can verify, if needed.

For simple tasks compress these fields to a short paragraph; do not fill sections with ceremony.
